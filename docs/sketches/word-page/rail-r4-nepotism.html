nepotism

/ˈnep.ə.tə.zəm/ /ˈnep.ə.tɪ.zəm/ /ˈnɛp.ə.tɪ.zəm/

noun

About the word

forms nepotisms

origin (noun) Borrowed from French népotisme, from Italian nepotismo, from Latin nepōs (“nephew”), a reference to the practice of popes appointing relatives (most often nephews) as cardinals (cardinal-nephew) during the Middle Ages and Renaissance. the rest of the origin · 33 characters
