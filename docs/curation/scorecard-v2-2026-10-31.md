## Per-choice rubric and denominators

Evaluate every proposed choice (lead included) from each primary attempt, including
choices omitted during assembly; separately report actual published placements.
For each record classify intended-meaning alignment as supported, wrong, or
insufficient supplied evidence. Metadata-only material may justify only a modest
claim supported by its supplied fields. Do not infer unseen plots, lyrics, images,
articles or poem contents. Correct record type and creator attribution are required.
Score every reason separately: grounded, unsupported/misattributed, or policy-
withheld/unassessable. A title/name match alone does not establish topical relevance.

Report all three meaning counts and all reason counts. Primary meaning rate =
supported / all proposed choices, with wrong AND insufficient-evidence choices in
the denominator. Also show scoring coverage = (supported + wrong) / all choices,
and the old assessable-only rate labelled secondary. Policy-withheld reasons are
unassessable, not automatically grounded. Zero choices has no percentage and cannot
pass usefulness. No sampling or dropping inconvenient, expired or failed cases.
If lawful evidence is no longer available, mark insufficient evidence; do not
archive prohibited payloads or reconstruct them from memory. Inspect before expiry
when possible, retaining only permitted judgments, identities/digests and metrics.

## Page outcome and quality GO

Every page is assessed as useful, justified empty, incorrect abstention, failed/
refused, or not assessed. A useful page has an appropriate exact sourced lead and
a coherent useful core; judge each supplementary choice too, not just the lead.
Optional sections remain empty if no offered material supports them. The bestiality
regression specifically requires recognizing an eligible supplied sense definition,
not a hard-coded output. A truly empty/unsafe packet may justify abstention, but
that is not a useful curated entry.

Quality GO requires ALL:
- Ten primary page outcomes recorded, with zero invented quotations or unauthorized
  source-content writes, and zero unsupported/misattributed published explanations.
- At least 90% supported meaning across ALL proposed choices by the denominator
  above; report absolute counts and uncertainty, never only assessable-subset 98%.
- Ten useful curated entries, including bestiality when suitable native definitions
  are supplied. Justified empty outcomes are honest partial progress, not completion
  of the owner's ten-useful-entry goal.
- Each publication has inspectable exact references, actual agent/model/persona/
  instruction/attempt/date, grounded reason/basis and authority. No fake human or
  consensus attribution. Failed/abstained/unchanged results remain distinguishable.

Report intermediate improvement even on NO-GO. Do not change thresholds after calls.
A changed rubric/instruction/cohort creates a new dated experiment and preserves
this version, its failures and the original 29 October report.

Quality and operational verdicts are separate. The subsequent authorized multi-day
window must observe a real due assessment without a page visit, a fresh repeat
with zero inference, unchanged assessment without duplicate publication, owner hold
preventing overwrite, honest offline deferral, and expiry with permitted citation
survival. Existing news-expiry evidence may be referenced by exact IDs/dates; do not
force a fresh news choice merely to fill a demonstration. Distinguish expired
selection/cache from provider fields explicitly allowed to remain durable.

Record every call including readiness, refused/failed/repair calls, artifact/config/
profile/instruction hashes, prompt/output tokens, elapsed inference, API wait,
available memory/swap and shared daily budget. Record developer evaluation minutes
prospectively; if unavailable say unmeasured. Useful published choices per machine
minute is reported, not inferred from successful calls alone. Small ten-page results
justify another bounded experiment, not corpus-wide autonomy or model superiority.

## Coverage report and evidence handling

Use mix dd.curation.coverage with DD_NO_OBAN=1 and PHX_SERVER unset.
The report reads existing attempts and the current published receipt; it requests
neither discovery nor inference and creates no rows. Historical counts.offered is
the eligible screen pool, candidates is the bounded packet, omitted gives exclusion
counts/reasons, result gives actual selected candidate IDs. Current shown/withheld
status is a later snapshot, not historical publication-time eligibility.
Total raw API inventory and historical developer scores were not captured in every
attempt: report unknown, not zero or an invented funnel. Do not sum snapshots across
attempts or claim every available source item was considered. Reports contain only
counts, statuses, permitted local IDs/hashes and dates, never provider text/reasons.
