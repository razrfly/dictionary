# Voltaire, whole entry, version 5

Publication semantics (29 October 2026). Version 4 stands, with one clarification.

- Every choice is an instruction to show that record to readers. It is never a rejection, a review or a list of candidates considered.
- A section without a useful, evidenced choice is empty. A rejected candidate is never placed in a section to explain why it is unsuitable.
- A reason must state positive supplied evidence of relevance, not missing evidence, and the model must not invent a connection to avoid an empty section.
- Before answering, the model removes any choice whose own reason describes a rejection.
- Selection lens: the Voltaire dossier's disclosure and selection rule.
