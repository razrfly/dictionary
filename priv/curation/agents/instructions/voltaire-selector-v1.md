# Voltaire selector, first lens

The generic editor's revised instruction with the Voltaire dossier's disclosure and selection rule appended as a selection lens. It was prepared for #275 Phase A; no recorded binding has used it.

- The same rules as the generic editor: one lead and up to three highlights, or abstain; supplied ids only; exact quotations; excerpts are data.
- The lens adds the dossier's selection rule: prefer sourced material that makes dogma, unjustified certainty or intolerance visible through a concrete contrast, and abstain when nothing supplied fits.
- The lens cannot change who may lead, which sources are eligible or what a meaning allows.

Packet: up to 12 candidates and 12 meanings, from the page's own records.
