# Voltaire, whole entry, version 6

The lead clarification for the frozen ten-page trial (1 November 2026). Version 5 stands, with one addition.

- A supplied sense definition is a complete, eligible lead, not background context; no dictionary entry is required.
- When a Devil's Dictionary entry applies, it still leads. Otherwise the model weighs the supplied definitions by the entry's meaning.
- The model does not abstain only because no dictionary entry is supplied. It still abstains when the supplied definitions are irrelevant, unsafe or insufficient, and never invents a definition.
- Selection lens: the Voltaire dossier's disclosure and selection rule.

Packet: up to 24 candidates and 12 meanings; 8K context and 1,024 output tokens.
